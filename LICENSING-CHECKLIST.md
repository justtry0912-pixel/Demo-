# Commercial Rights and Delivery Checklist

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice. Rules and contract needs vary by location and project.

## Before production
- [ ] Client has permission to use supplied logos, product photos, footage and brand assets.
- [ ] Product claims, comparisons, endorsements and testimonials are accurate and approved.
- [ ] Music license covers the intended platform, territory, duration and paid-ad use.
- [ ] Fonts, stock footage, templates, 3D models and plugins permit the intended commercial use.
- [ ] AI tool terms and the specific plan permit the intended use; check restrictions and disclosure rules.
- [ ] Model/property releases are secured when required.
- [ ] No competitor ad or creator work is copied.

## Before delivery
- [ ] Correct product, packaging, spelling, logo and CTA
- [ ] Approved copy and substantiation on file
- [ ] Required ad disclosures included
- [ ] Platform-specific ad policies checked
- [ ] Music and third-party asset license records archived
- [ ] Final exports tested for dimensions, frame rate, audio and visual defects
- [ ] Client approval documented
- [ ] Source files and third-party assets are handled according to the contract and license terms

## When selling this toolkit
- [ ] Describe exactly what buyers receive
- [ ] State that third-party subscriptions and assets may be separate
- [ ] Include a clear license and refund/support policy
- [ ] Avoid implying endorsement or affiliation with AI/tool vendors
- [ ] Review platform and local legal requirements for your offer
