# Prompt 02 — Concepts and Storyboard

Use after the brief is approved.

---
Using the approved brief, create **three meaningfully different ad concepts**. Avoid simply changing colors or adjectives.

For each concept include:
- Concept title and one-sentence idea
- First 1–2 second hook
- Audience insight and rationale
- Visual metaphor / product reveal
- Palette, type treatment, lighting and motion behavior
- Suggested sound design (no unlicensed track assumptions)
- Risks or assets needed

Then recommend one route and create a storyboard for [RUNTIME] seconds. Use a table with:
- Timecode
- Shot/scene
- Composition and subject
- Motion/action and easing
- On-screen text
- Voiceover (if useful)
- Sound cue
- Transition to next scene
- Required asset

Make all time ranges add up to the runtime. Keep text readable on a phone and respect platform safe areas. Include a CTA/end card. Give a no-voiceover version. Mark placeholders clearly and do not fabricate evidence or product claims.
