# Prompt 01 — Creative Brief

Copy into Claude after adding your client/product information.

---
Act as a senior advertising creative strategist. Help me create a motion graphics ad from the information below.

**Brand/product:** [name + category]  
**Audience:** [who should care]  
**Objective:** [awareness / product launch / traffic / leads / sales]  
**Platform:** [Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube / website / other]  
**Runtime:** [6 / 10 / 15 / 30 seconds]  
**Aspect ratio:** [9:16 / 1:1 / 16:9]  
**Brand personality:** [premium / playful / futuristic / minimal / bold]  
**Main benefit:** [verified benefit]  
**Proof available:** [evidence, review, demonstration, or none]  
**Offer and CTA:** [details, or none]  
**Brand colors/fonts:** [details]  
**Available assets:** [product photos, logo, footage, 3D model, etc.]  
**Production tool:** [After Effects / Blender / Remotion / other / undecided]  
**Budget/deadline constraints:** [details]  
**Must include / must avoid:** [details]

Tasks:
1. Identify missing information that could materially affect the ad. Ask up to 5 concise questions, or state assumptions if I want you to proceed.
2. Summarize the brief in one paragraph.
3. Define the audience insight, single-minded message, desired action and one success metric.
4. Flag unsupported claims or missing asset rights.
5. Suggest a visual direction, palette, typography character, motion language and sound direction.
Do not invent claims or guarantee ad performance.
