# Selling Guide — Motion Ad Studio Kit

This kit can support a service business or a digital download. It is not a guarantee of demand or income.

## Position the offer around outcomes
Sell a clear deliverable and workflow, not “AI magic.” Examples:
- **Starter:** one 6–10 second motion ad, one format, one revision round
- **Campaign:** one main ad plus agreed cutdowns and two revision rounds
- **Creative system:** several concept routes, a campaign visual system and a defined batch of ads

Define exact runtimes, formats, number of concepts, revision limits, delivery date, source-file terms and exclusions before quoting. Set prices based on your skill, complexity, client market, time and asset costs.

## Suggested workflow
1. Qualify the client's objective, audience, product and deadline.
2. Collect brand assets and written approval for claims.
3. Send concepts and storyboard for approval before production.
4. Produce a low-cost preview/watermarked review file where appropriate.
5. Include the agreed revision rounds.
6. QA and deliver agreed exports and source files.
7. Archive approvals, license records and final versions.

## Build a credible portfolio
- Create 3–5 original spec ads for fictional brands or products you have permission to feature.
- Label spec work clearly; do not imply the fictional brand hired you.
- Show a short breakdown: brief → storyboard → motion details → final.
- Use only assets with suitable commercial rights.
- Make demos genuinely different across industries and styles.

## Digital product packaging
A paid download may include:
- This prompt kit and brief templates
- A concise setup guide
- A few original example briefs and storyboards
- A sample project you created and can license
- Version history and support terms

Add a product description that accurately lists what is included and what customers need separately (Claude access, editing software, paid assets, rendering tools). State license scope, refund policy, update policy and support boundaries. Do not imply affiliation with Anthropic, Adobe or other brands.

## Basic product page outline
1. Specific problem it solves
2. Who it is for / not for
3. Preview of actual files and examples
4. Exact contents and requirements
5. How the workflow works
6. License and usage terms
7. Limitations and FAQ

## Important
Do not promise guaranteed sales, virality, conversion lift or return on ad spend. Avoid fake testimonials, copied competitor work and unsubstantiated performance claims.
